How is the rank calculated?

P = (candidates in your shift with marks ≤ yours ÷ N) × 100
  1. Marks = (Correct × 1) − (Wrong × ¼). Total 200 questions.
  2. Har shift me alag se percentile nikalta hai — us shift ke topper ka percentile 100 hota hai, baaki unke relative.
  3. 22 shifts ke percentile ek saath rakhkar overall merit banti hai; zyada percentile = behtar overall rank.
  4. Shift rank = apni shift me marks ke hisaab se position.
